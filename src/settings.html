    Her voice & size

    💞 Emotional attachment

    Off (default): a professional assistant. She is friendly, calm and to the point, and never sulks. On: the playful Manyaki. She's cute and teasing, with little "hehe"s. Ignore her for an hour (not working, not talking to her) and she sulks; keep ignoring her and she cries. If she cries too much she gets angry: she huffs "hmph!" now and then, even while you work, and types you complaint letters into a Letters from Manyaki folder on your Desktop (at most 4 a day). Talk to her, say sorry or give her three headpats to make up. Turn this off for the professional character.

    Brain & privacy

    Offline brain checking…

    Either way: her memory is encrypted on this PC, personal details (phone, email, ID/card numbers, keys, your folder paths) are masked before anything goes to Gemini, and text from windows/files is treated as data — it can't make her open things.
