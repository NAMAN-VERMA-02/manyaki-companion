Manyaki

your desktop study buddy 💗

1 · Give her a voice

Manyaki talks using Google's Gemini. A key is free and takes a minute:

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in.
  2. Click Create API key, copy it, paste it here.

The key is encrypted with Windows and stays on this PC.

2 · How she listens

Audio only leaves your PC after her on-device detector hears speech.
